Questions, answered

Do I need a paid Canva account?

No. A free Canva account is enough to open the template and edit it.

What do I actually receive?

A PDF with a button that opens your editable Canva template. This is a digital product, so nothing is shipped.

What size is it?

A4 portrait. You can print it at home or at a print shop, or share it digitally.

Can I resell or share the template?

It is for personal use only. Please don't resell, share or redistribute the template or its files.

Will colors match my printer?

Colors can look a little different depending on your screen and printer.
