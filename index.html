Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need a paid Canva account?

No. A free Canva account is enough to open the templates and edit them.

What do I actually receive?

A PDF with a button that opens your editable Canva template. The bundle gives you two PDFs, one for each set. This is a digital product, so nothing is shipped.

What's the difference between the bundle and buying separately?

Nothing in the files. The bundle has the same two sets for ₱299 instead of ₱398.

What size is it?

A4 portrait. You can print at home or at a print shop, or share it digitally.

Can I resell or share the templates?

They are for personal use only. Please don't resell, share or redistribute the templates or their files.

Will colors match my printer?

Colors can look a little different depending on your screen and printer.
